### Lens 4 — LLM-as-judge framework pick: **A− (correct pick; defense incomplete)**

G-Eval rubric-as-prompt is defensible. Three caveats the brief should call out:
- G-Eval has documented bias toward in-family generations (the NeurIPS '23 paper itself raises this). Mandates cross-family judge — brief mentions this in the §2 prose tail but it should be in the row decision.
- DeepEval ≠ G-Eval. DeepEval implements G-Eval among many; brief conflates them at the row level.
- RAGAS "Track (RAG-specific)" is fine, but regatta's L4 gate over agent-generated patches *does* have a retrieval component (codebase context); RAGAS metrics for context-precision may be load-bearing for `cost-governor` cost-vs-quality tradeoffs.

### Lens 5 — Sandbox runtime maturity: **B+ (data cited; sources thin)**

Cold-start and cost data is cited from one vendor-affiliated comparison (Northflank blog). Independent benchmarks (Superagent cited; otherwise sparse) would harden the "Adopt E2B / secondary Daytona" call. Modal "3× normal" is non-falsifiable without baseline. Blaxel "25ms" is from vendor marketing, marked "Track — too new" correctly.

**Action:** flag the E2B / Daytona cost numbers as "needs validation spike" or pin to an independent benchmark.

### Lens 6 — Skill-marketplace strategy: **B (dual-publish call is right; maintenance tax ignored)**

"Dual-publish Claude Skill + MCP server" is the right strategic call but:
- Brief doesn't quantify the maintenance burden of two manifests + two release cadences + two install-instructions.
- Brief doesn't address what happens when Anthropic Skills and the MCP registry diverge (auth model, capabilities surface, slash-command bindings).
- "Single-channel = invisibility risk" is asserted; the symmetric risk ("dual-channel = double-bug-surface") isn't.

### Lens 7 — Plan-language standards: **A− (decisions are sound; framing should be inverted)**

"Reject as primary, steal BT composition semantics" is the correct call. The framing should be "what stays (CUE), what we steal (BT subtrees), what we reject (everything else)" rather than the current implicit "pick a substrate". §5 reads as if regatta is shopping; in reality regatta has CUE in production already. Reframe.

### Lens 8 — CRDT 24-mo prediction: **B (realistic-ish; agent-state-vs-text-CRDT mismatch unaddressed)**

Yjs and Automerge are mature for **text** and JSON-like structures. Agent state involves semantic conflicts (two agents propose conflicting refactors) that CRDTs cannot resolve without an additional layer — they merge syntactically. The brief asserts "collapse P6+P9 into one library" without addressing the semantic-merge gap. The Electric blog (Apr 2026) cited in §6 covers the *peer-pattern* but not the *semantic-conflict* dimension.

**Action:** add one row to §6 stating the open problem: "CRDT merges syntactically; agent disagreement is semantic; need a layered protocol (CRDT for state, judge for semantic merges)".
