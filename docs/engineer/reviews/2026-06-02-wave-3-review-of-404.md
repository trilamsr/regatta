### Lens 3 — OTel + OpenInference + OpenLLMetry adoption — schema commit or hand-wave: **FAIL — hand-wave + wrong-namespace**

The brief commits to "borrow the LLM-span attribute schema" without naming **which** schema. OpenInference (`llm.token_count.prompt`) and OTel GenAI semconv (`gen_ai.usage.input_tokens`) are different namespaces. The brief's only specific attribute names (Insight 4, §4.2) are **OpenInference**. W6 emits **GenAI semconv**. Either: (a) the brief should name OTel GenAI semconv as the schema regatta picked and frame the open question as cross-namespace compatibility, or (b) the brief is recommending a namespace switch — in which case it owes a much bigger argument than one paragraph. F2.

### Lens 4 — Durable-exec convergence (step-memoization beating event-sourcing): **PARTIAL — claim is mostly right but mis-sourced and contradicts in-repo decision**

The technical claim (`substrate_events` matches Inngest's `step.run` ergonomics) is plausible — the W9 redteam reached a similar conclusion. The framing ("memoization > event-sourcing") is over-stated:

- **Inngest's memoization** stores step results keyed by step id (skip-if-exists semantics).
- **Temporal's event-sourcing** stores the full event history + replays deterministically.
- **Restate's journal model** is closer to memoization than to event-sourcing but is not identical to either.

The brief presents the spectrum as binary (memoization-or-replay). It is not. F5.

### Lens 5 — Gate-blocks-merge convergence — defensible differentiation: **PARTIAL — the differentiation is defensible if narrowed**

"Regatta gates on methodology, neighbors gate on quality/style" is **defensible** for (Braintrust, GitHub merge queue, Renovate) — none of those three target falsifiability-relevant methodology. But the survey omits DeepEval / promptfoo / ragas, which **do** target methodology-shaped LLM-eval gates (bias detection, hallucination detection, prompt-leakage). Regatta still has a wedge — its four gates target *empirical-research* methodology (p-hack, power, leakage, stat-test), not LLM-output methodology — but the wedge needs the narrower framing. F6.
